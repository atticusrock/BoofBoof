# Boof Boof — Initial Public Build

Boof Boof is a free menu bar clipboard history for macOS.

### Included

- Local history for plain text, web links, and PNG/JPEG/HEIC/WebP/TIFF images.
- Search, restore to clipboard, pin, delete, pause/resume, and clear controls.
- Configurable retention and unpinned-entry limits.
- Universal app for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

### Default limits

- 50 unpinned entries, retained for 10 days.
- 1 MiB maximum text item.
- 10 MiB per image, 100 MiB total image storage, 40 megapixels per image.

### Important

This build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple. macOS may display a Gatekeeper warning when opening an internet-downloaded copy. It is free to download and use; no subscription or account is required.
