# Boof Boof 1.1.2

- The new Boof Boof app icon now appears next to the title in the menu bar popover.
- Free universal build for macOS 13 or later on Intel and Apple Silicon.

To update, quit Boof Boof, open the DMG, drag the app into Applications, and choose **Replace** if prompted. Clipboard history and settings stay in your local user data.

This build is ad hoc signed. macOS may show a security warning on first launch because it has not been notarized by Apple.
