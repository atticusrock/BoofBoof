# Boof Boof 1.1.0

- **Files in history.** Copy a single regular file in Finder. Boof Boof keeps a local copy under the original filename, ready to restore and paste as a file. Limits are 50 MiB per file, 100 MiB per video, and 500 MiB combined, including pinned files. Supported images retain their own storage limits.
- **Quick Switcher.** Enable it in Settings → History Access. Press the configurable shortcut (Control–Shift–V by default) to open a row of previews. Press the same shortcut again to cycle, or use the arrow keys. Press Return to copy the selection and Command–V to paste it in the previous app.
- **Media previews.** Quick Switcher shows thumbnails for supported images and local media, with a file details view when a thumbnail is unavailable.
- **Interface.** Settings are arranged in one column without scrolling. History cards have direct pin and delete controls, and bulk cleanup uses two dedicated buttons.

Free for macOS 13 or later. The universal installer supports Intel and Apple Silicon. Clipboard history stays on your Mac.

The app is ad hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. macOS may show a warning on first launch.
